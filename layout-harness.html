Start here what this canvas is

JustClarify is a browser extension: highlight anything on any page and it explains it right there — free, on your own device, open source. Everything on this canvas is rendered with the extension's real CSS, so what you see here is what ships.

Step 1 — highlight & choose two per page; the arrow reveals more

Highlight text → the diamond appears under it → click it and this bar opens on Explain + Expand. Highlight a single word and Define joins that first page. The › arrow pages onward — Fact-check + Text area, then Example — and the highlight itself can invite more: Translate when the text isn't English, Summarize when it's long. Pick an action: the diamond slides under it, the label shimmers while it thinks, then the panel below grows to fit the reply. Click the same action again to dismiss.

Ambient panel opt-in floating workspace, streams live

An optional companion that floats at the page's edge. While a question is in flight it shows a miniature of the answer card — the answering model's mark, the quoted highlight, then the text as it streams. Settles back to topic chips when done.

Engine card what answers, and why — honest diagnostics

Where the power for prompts comes from. On-device first: Chrome's built-in model — free, private, offline. It needs 16GB RAM, 4GB VRAM and 22GB free disk — all three — and reports a bare "unavailable" without saying which failed; the blocked state names the bar and points at the fix (your own key via the AI Gateway).

Step 4 — fact-check one claim behind the › arrow on the bar

Highlight a claim, page the bar to Fact-check, and the verdict lands in the same panel. Evidence first: a free lookup against published ClaimReview rulings (PolitiFact, Snopes, FactCheck.org…), then a search-grounded model only for claims nobody has ruled on. Every verdict carries its sources — never an opinion dressed up as a fact.

Step 5 — fact-check a page, transcript, live verdicts land as they resolve

The same checker, pointed at a whole article, a YouTube transcript, or live tab audio. Claims are extracted on-device for free, then verified in parallel — each verdict appears the moment it resolves instead of waiting for the slowest, and timestamps seek the video.

Step 2 — the answer key line pinned, topic tagged

What lands after you pick an action: the answer streams into the panel, and the model marks its one load-bearing sentence with [[KEY]] and tags the subject with [[TOPIC]]. The key line gets the red rule; the topic becomes a chip. Malformed or unclosed tags from a weak model are stripped, never shown raw.

Step 3 — first run on-device consent, then the one-time download bar

What a brand-new user sees the first time an answer would need Chrome's built-in model downloaded (and no key is set): JustClarify asks first. Accept, and the one-time ~4GB download shows as a labelled bar; after that, every answer is instant and uses the normal dot loader.

Set up on-device AI?

JustClarify can answer right on your machine — free, private, and it works offline. Chrome downloads the built-in model once (about 4GB); after that it's instant. Nothing you highlight ever leaves your device.
