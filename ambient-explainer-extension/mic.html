Let JustClarify hear you on every site.

Websites each control their own microphone access, and plenty of them switch it off entirely. Granting it here instead attaches the permission to JustClarify, so hold to talk works the same everywhere.

  • Nothing is recorded unless you are holding Shift.
  • The recording ends the moment you let go.
  • Audio goes to the speech service to become text, and is not stored.

You can undo this any time from Chrome's site settings for this extension.
